Website check

ApeScope can look for the token's contract address on its project website and on GitHub, and check the domain age. Project websites can be on any domain, so this needs access to all websites. The pages are only fetched as text, without your cookies, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

The same access is used for a custom Solana RPC URL from the settings.
